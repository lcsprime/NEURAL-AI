Technology solutions for every layer of your business

01

IT Strategy & Advisory

Many organizations operate with a patchwork of technology decisions made under pressure. We bring structure — a clear audit of where you are, a frank assessment of what isn't working, and a prioritized roadmap for where to invest next.

This isn't a lengthy engagement that gathers dust. It's a sharp, practical plan you can act on within weeks.

What you'll get

Technology landscape audit across all systems
Gap analysis and risk identification
Prioritized 12-month IT roadmap
Budget guidance and vendor recommendations
Executive presentation ready to share
02

Cloud Migration & Management

Moving to the cloud is one of the most valuable — and most mishandled — IT projects a business can undertake. We make it methodical: no surprise downtime, no data left behind, no ballooning costs.

We work with AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, and we'll tell you which is right for your workload — not which one we have a preferred deal with.

What you'll get

Cloud readiness assessment
Migration architecture and sequencing plan
Managed cutover with zero-disruption approach
Cost optimization configuration post-migration
Ongoing cloud management (optional retainer)

What you'll get

Neural AI Computing is an IT consulting firm based in Irving, TX. We help organizations plan, implement, and optimize the technology that powers their business — with focus, experience, and precision.

Our story

Why we started Neural AI Computing

Neural AI Computing was built on a straightforward idea: growing businesses deserve the same quality of IT consulting that large enterprises get — without the inflated timelines, junior-heavy teams, or generic recommendations.

We work closely with each client, starting from a clear-eyed assessment of where things stand today, and building toward technology that genuinely fits their organization — not technology that was convenient for us to recommend.

We're a firm in the early stages of growth. That means every client relationship matters deeply to us, and every engagement gets our full focus.
